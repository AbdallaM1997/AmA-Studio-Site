## 1. Create the account
On your phone: TikTok app → **Profile** → **Sign up** (or, if you already use TikTok: your profile → your username at the top → **Add account** → **Sign up**).

- **Sign up with:** email `info@amastudios.net` (a studio email, not a personal one, so the account belongs to the studio).
- **Birthday:** your own real birthday (the account owner's; TikTok uses it for age checks, it isn't shown).
- **Username:** `amastudio.games` (same as Instagram and Facebook). If it's taken, try `amastudio` or `amastudios`.
  You can only change the username once every 30 days, so get it right the first time.

If you already have a personal TikTok, you can switch between both accounts in the app.

## 2. Switch to a Business account
Profile → ☰ menu → **Settings and privacy** → **Account** → **Switch to Business Account**.

- Category: `Games` (or `Gaming` / `Software & Apps`, whichever is in the list).
- This gives you the website link in your bio, analytics, and the Business Suite.

**About music:** a Business account can only use the **Commercial Music Library** (sounds cleared for brands). That's the right choice for a studio: songs from the full library can get a brand's video muted or taken down. The commercial library still has a **Trending** tab, so pick popular sounds from there.

## 4. Settings worth doing once
Profile → ☰ → **Settings and privacy**.

- **Security → 2-step verification:** on (email + an authenticator app if you can).
- **Privacy → Comments:** Everyone. Turn on **Filter spam and offensive comments**.
- **Privacy → Duet / Stitch / Downloads:** Everyone. People reacting to and sharing your clips is free reach.
- Save the login in your password manager, and keep a second trusted person who knows how to get in.

## 5. First videos (before you tell anyone)
Post three so a new visitor sees a full first row, then keep going. TikTok shows the newest first.

How to post each one: **+** → **Upload** → pick the file → **Next**.
- **Sound:** **Add sound** → Commercial Music Library → Trending. Keep the volume low enough to feel calm, it's a puzzle game.
- **Caption:** paste the text below. Keep it to one or two lines so the video stays clear.
- **Cover:** pick a frame where the board is full and clear.
- **Who can watch:** Everyone. Leave Duet / Stitch on.
- **More options → Disclose post content:** leave off (it's your own game, not a paid partnership).

The videos keep the headline at the top and the board in the middle, clear of TikTok's buttons on the right. The caption covers the AmA logo at the bottom for a moment; that's fine.

## 6. Keep it going
- **Rhythm:** about 3 videos a week (Sun, Tue, Thu evenings, 8–11 pm Cairo time). See `marketing/PLAN.md` for the content pillars.
- **Re-use everything:** each video goes to TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels and YouTube Shorts. Post the same file, change only the caption.
- **Reply to comments with a video** (tap the comment → reply with video). "Which group does this card go in?" questions make easy, fast videos.
- **New clips:** record a level in Unity (Game view at 1080×1920) into `marketing/clips/`, then ask Claude: *"Make a TikTok from marketing/clips/level-15.mp4 with the headline 'One move left'."*
- **Phone-made videos are fine too:** the desk, a sketch, a funny bug. Honest and small does well on TikTok.
- Check **Analytics** (☰ → **Business Suite**) after a month: which videos were watched to the end, and at what time your followers are online.
